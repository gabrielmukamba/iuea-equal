Proposed merit rubric

A balanced model for selecting campus ambassadors on holistic excellence.

CategoryWeightMeasurable indicators
Innovation & Problem Solving30%Practical campus or community project, technical creativity, feasibility
Leadership & Communication25%Public speaking clarity, teamwork record, ability to unite diverse students
Academic & Intellectual Competence20%Critical thinking, Q&A depth, consistent academic discipline
Character & Community Impact15%Integrity, peer mentorship, volunteerism, ethical conduct
Stage Presence & Presentation10%Confidence, poise, cultural representation, professionalism

This rubric is a proposal for discussion. It is not an official IUEA policy.
