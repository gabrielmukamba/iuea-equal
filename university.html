What students should know about IUEA, its competitions and how to get involved.

IUEA at a glance

The International University of East Africa is a private, non-profit university in Kansanga, Kampala, chartered by Uganda's National Council for Higher Education. It was established in 2010. Its motto is "Learning to Succeed".

Faculties

Science and Technology, Business and Management, Engineering, and Law. IUEA also runs a Foundation Studies Department and a Centre of Languages and Professional Skills. Check iuea.ac.ug for the current list of programmes.

Mr and Miss IUEA

Mr and Miss IUEA is the university's annual pageant. Voting runs on the official IUEA Ballot platform, which uses email verification, shows live status and describes its safeguards on the site.

IUEA EQUAL is not part of the pageant or the ballot. We propose clear, published judging criteria so that contestants and students know how recognition works before an event begins. See our proposed rubric.
