Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

IUEA EQUAL is an independent student initiative and is not an official IUEA system.

What we collect

When you submit a project, feedback or a chat message, the text you type, including the name you enter, is stored with Google Firebase. We do not ask for your email address or phone number. Please do not type them.

Who can see it

Chat messages are public to anyone who opens the chat page. Projects and feedback become public after the founder approves them.

On your device

Your browser keeps a random ID and the name you used in the chat, so your own messages appear on the right. Nothing else is stored.

How it is used

To connect students and improve fairness recommendations. We do not sell data or run advertising.

Removal

Email gabrielmukamba7@gmail.com to ask for a project, feedback or message to be deleted.
